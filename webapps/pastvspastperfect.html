Grammar Focus: Past vs. Past Perfect

Simple Past (Verb-2 / -ed): Used for a completed action in the past, or a sequence of past events happening one right after another.

Past Perfect (had + Past Participle / Verb-3): Used to show that an action happened before another action in the past. It helps clarify the order of events.

  • Sequence (Both Simple Past): "When she heard the loud noise, she dropped her bag." (Immediate reaction)
  • Out of Order (Past Perfect + Simple Past): "By the time we arrived, the train had left." (The train leaving happened FIRST).
